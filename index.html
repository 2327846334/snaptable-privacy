Privacy Policy

SnapTable — Screenshot to Excel & OCR · Effective date: 2026-10-14

English
The short version: SnapTable performs all text recognition inside your browser. Your images are never uploaded and never leave your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

1. What we collect

Nothing. SnapTable has no server that receives your data, no account system, and no telemetry. The extension does not contain analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind.

2. How your images are processed

When you recognise an image or screenshot, the image is loaded into the extension's own background page and processed by an OCR engine (Tesseract.js, a WebAssembly build) that runs entirely on your device. The image is never transmitted anywhere. Closing the browser discards it.

This is the reason the extension bundles its language model files rather than downloading them at runtime: nothing about your images or your usage is observable to anyone, including us.

3. What is stored on your device

All of the following lives in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and can be erased at any time from the extension's settings page:

  • Your language and recognition-mode preferences
  • A short history of recent recognitions (text excerpts only, capped at 25 entries)
  • A daily counter for the free tier
  • Your licence key and activation state, if you purchase Pro

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

5. Third parties

The free version contacts no third party at all.

If you purchase the Pro version, two limited interactions occur, both only because a payment is involved:

  • Your browser is sent to our payment provider's checkout page. Their handling of your payment details is governed by their own privacy policy.
  • When you activate, the licence key you entered is sent to the payment provider's validation endpoint to confirm it is valid. Nothing else is sent — no image, no usage data, no identifier beyond the key itself.

We do not sell or share personal data with anyone, because we do not have any.

6. Children's privacy

SnapTable is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled — for example, if a cloud OCR option were added — this page and the extension's store listing will be updated together before that version ships, and the effective date above will change.

二、图片是如何处理的

当你识别图片或截图时，图片会被载入扩展自己的后台页面，由 Tesseract.js（WebAssembly 版 OCR 引擎）在你的设备上完成处理。图片不会被传输到任何地方，关闭浏览器即被丢弃。

这也解释了为什么扩展把语言模型文件**打包在安装包内**而不是运行时下载： 这样你的图片和使用方式对任何人都不可观测，包括我们。

三、本机保存了什么

以下内容保存在浏览器的扩展本地存储（chrome.storage.local）中， 可随时在设置页一键清除：

  • 你的语言与识别模式偏好
  • 最近的识别历史（仅文字片段，上限 25 条）
  • 免费版每日次数计数
  • 仅在购买 Pro 后才有：许可证密钥与激活状态

卸载扩展会一并删除全部内容。
